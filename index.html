Yunqing Zhao

I am an AI Researcher at Tencent Hy in Singapore.

My research focuses on multimodal reasoning and generation, with an emphasis on efficient learning and reliable behavior. My work has been published at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV, and in TIP and TPAMI.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist at TikTok / ByteDance. I received my PhD from SUTD in 2024, advised by Ngai-Man Cheung, and received the Outstanding Thesis Award (one of 3 theses selected university-wide).

Research

I'm interested in multimodal models that reason, use tools, and generate visual content. My research explores how these models learn and adapt efficiently, and how to evaluate and improve their reliability. Some papers are highlighted.

Let ViT Speak: Generative Language-Image Pre-training

Yan Fang*, Mengcheng Lan*, Zilong Huang†, Weixian Lei, Yunqing Zhao, Yujie Zhong, Yingchen Yu, Qi She, Yao Zhao, Yunchao Wei†
European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), Malmö, Sweden, 2026.
paper / project page / code / models / poster / bibtex

GenLIP pretrains the vision encoder as a language generator: the ViT reads image patches and learns to predict the accompanying text. This autoregressive objective aligns visual pretraining with LLMs, without a contrastive loss or a separate text decoder.

Training integrated into mlfoundations/open_clip, with initial encoder support in Hugging Face's timm.
